Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Nexora Hub. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website.

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors who use Nexora Hub to explore job opportunities, read career resources, discover digital products, or contact us.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and the contents of a message sent through a contact form.

We may also collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, and general usage data, where applicable through server logs, analytics, security tools, or similar technologies.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through our website unless it is specifically requested and necessary.

2. How We Use Information

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • Understand how visitors use our pages.
  • Protect the website against spam, fraud, and misuse.
  • Process purchases through payment providers, where available.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nexora Hub or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, measure website usage, maintain security, or display advertisements.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

Nexora Hub may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if our website is approved and the service is implemented.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website or other websites, subject to the relevant service settings, consent requirements, and applicable law.

Google advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to show advertisements to users based on their visits to this website or other websites. Users can learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites through Google's advertising and privacy resources.

For information about Google's data practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

If personalised advertising or other advertising technologies are enabled, we will follow the applicable Google policies and legal requirements, including consent requirements where relevant.

Our website may link to external job boards, employer websites, digital product platforms, payment providers, or other third-party services.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and practices. Nexora Hub does not control the privacy practices of external websites, so we encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information or making payments.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information as a general business practice. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, with your direction, where required by law, or where necessary to protect our rights and prevent fraud.

The services actually used on this website will determine which information is processed by third parties.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of certain personal information.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Nexora Hub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other safeguards are required by law. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us.
